Every tokenized asset, continuously verified.

VeriLex watches a portfolio of tokenized real-world assets the way a registrar watches a land register: every custodian attestation checked against on-chain supply, every verdict scored under a published policy and entered into a public on-chain record. When an asset falls into breach, its own token freezes transfers until compliance is restored.

How the register is kept

firstAttestEach custodian publishes reserve and legal attestations for its asset.
secondFetchA Chainlink CRE workflow collects attestations with DON consensus and reads each token's supply on-chain.
thirdConvertReserves held in Singapore dollars are converted at a Chainlink Data Feed price — never a hardcoded rate.
fourthExamineA versioned policy scores seven checks per asset; an analyst writes the risks and the remedy in plain language.
fifthEnter & enforceVerdict, score and trail hash are entered in the ComplianceRegistry; gated tokens freeze on breach.
Entries on this page were produced by actually running the VeriLex pipeline — custodian attestations, the policy engine, the feed conversion and the on-chain registry on a local Sepolia-masquerade chain — by contracts/scripts/submit-demo-reports.ts in the repository. Custodian attestations are simulated (real trustees expose similar APIs); the contracts, the scoring, the conversion and the freeze are real and covered by 27 automated tests.
