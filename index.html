VeriLex — continuous compliance for tokenized RWAs

A Chainlink CRE-powered agent continuously verifies a whole portfolio of tokenized real-world assets: custodian reserves, licences, legal documents, audits, sanctions screening and attestation freshness. Every verdict gets a 0–100 compliance score and a hashed reasoning trail anchored on-chain — and when an asset breaches, its own token freezes transfers until compliance is restored.

How it works

1 · AttestCustodians publish reserve & legal attestations per asset.
2 · FetchThe CRE workflow pulls attestations with DON consensus and reads each token's supply on-chain.
3 · ConvertForeign-currency reserves (SGD) are converted with a Chainlink Data Feed price.
4 · EvaluateA versioned policy engine scores 7+ checks and an analyst narrates risks & remediation.
5 · Anchor & enforceVerdict, score and trail hash land in ComplianceRegistry; gated tokens freeze on BREACH.
